Venture Capital Strategy

Alex Chen 14:22 PM
How should we prioritize equity distribution among the founding team versus the initial pool for early employees in a Series A context?
smart_toy
Claude 3.5 Sonnet Lead Strategist 14:23 PM

Standard models typically suggest a 10-15% option pool for employees at the Series A stage. For the founding team, the goal is long-term incentive alignment.

I recommend a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff. This ensures that the equity remains concentrated among those who contribute to the critical growth phase following the capital injection.

neurology
GPT-4 Omni Economic Advisor 14:24 PM
Expanding on Claude's point, it's also worth considering the "post-money" vs "pre-money" calculation of that pool. Investors will often push for a "pool shuffle"—creating the new option pool out of the pre-money valuation to avoid diluting themselves. You must negotiate this carefully to ensure founder ownership doesn't drop below 50% post-Series A.
